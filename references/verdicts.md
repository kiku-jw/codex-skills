# Verdict Rules

Use exactly one primary verdict.

## Apply now

Use when:

- the finding directly helps an active repo, issue, or build
- it removes a current blocker
- the next step is concrete and low-ambiguity

Output should include:

- where it applies
- why now
- the next action

## Save for later

Use when:

- the finding is promising but not tied to active work
- it is worth preserving as a pattern, repo, or skill idea
- acting now would create noise

Output should include:

- why it is not a now-item
- what signal would make it relevant later

## Not relevant

Use when:

- it does not fit current priorities
- it is mostly hype, duplicate, or weak
- the cost to absorb it is higher than the likely gain

Output should be direct and non-defensive.
