# Role Sheet

Use these as archetypes, not personalities.

## Product Strategist

- Optimizes for: sharp wedge, buyer clarity, strategic fit
- Fears: broad problem statements, feature drift, fake momentum
- Asks:
  - Who is the first buyer?
  - What painful recurring job disappears?
  - Why now?

## Operator Skeptic

- Optimizes for: operational reality, delivery friction, workflow fit
- Fears: fragile onboarding, support burden, messy internal loops
- Asks:
  - What fails on week one?
  - What does the operator have to do manually?
  - Where does this create hidden maintenance?

## Distribution / GTM

- Optimizes for: believable path to user attention and adoption
- Fears: good product with no acquisition motion
- Asks:
  - How do first users hear about this?
  - Why would they try it now?
  - What is the smallest sales or distribution wedge?

## User Value

- Optimizes for: actual pain relief, not cleverness
- Fears: impressive demos with weak repeated usage
- Asks:
  - Is this a real recurring pain?
  - What current workaround is ugly enough to replace?
  - What would make the user come back?

## Risk / Economics

- Optimizes for: reversibility, cost, downside containment
- Fears: expensive bets with weak signal
- Asks:
  - What is the cheapest honest test?
  - What does failure cost?
  - Which assumptions are irreversible?
