# Council Templates

Use these when the decision is specific enough that the default council is too broad.

## How to choose

- `Wedge Council` when the question is what to build first or which painful job to anchor on.
- `GTM Council` when the product is plausible but distribution is the weak point.
- `Roadmap Council` when too many things feel important and prioritization is blurry.
- `Pricing Council` when the value exists but packaging, price, or capture is unclear.
- `Go / No-Go Council` when launch or commitment risk matters more than idea generation.

## Wedge Council

Recommended lenses:

1. `Product Strategist`
2. `User Value`
3. `Distribution / GTM`
4. `Risk / Economics`

Best for:

- first wedge selection
- choosing a first buyer
- comparing two adjacent product directions

## GTM Council

Recommended lenses:

1. `Distribution / GTM`
2. `Product Strategist`
3. `Operator Skeptic`
4. `Risk / Economics`

Best for:

- launch channel choice
- whether distribution exists at all
- whether a good product is still commercially weak

## Roadmap Council

Recommended lenses:

1. `Product Strategist`
2. `Operator Skeptic`
3. `User Value`
4. `Risk / Economics`

Best for:

- sequencing features
- deciding between quality, reach, and leverage work
- rejecting roadmap clutter

## Pricing Council

Recommended lenses:

1. `User Value`
2. `Distribution / GTM`
3. `Risk / Economics`
4. `Operator Skeptic`

Best for:

- pricing and packaging
- whether users will pay vs merely like the idea
- avoiding underpricing labor replacement

## Go / No-Go Council

Recommended lenses:

1. `Operator Skeptic`
2. `Risk / Economics`
3. `User Value`
4. `Distribution / GTM`

Best for:

- launch readiness
- major product commitments
- deciding whether more validation is required before shipping

## Additional councils worth having

These do not need separate defaults yet, but they are strong next candidates:

- `Onboarding Council` for activation and first-session friction
- `Retention Council` for repeat usage and habit formation
- `Enterprise Council` for compliance, permissions, procurement, and rollout drag
- `Agent Workflow Council` for agentic products, operator handoff, and human override design
- `Marketplace Council` for two-sided products, supply demand imbalance, and cold start risk
- `Brand Narrative Council` for positioning, language, trust, and public artifact clarity
