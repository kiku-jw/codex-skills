# Common AI Writing Patterns

Cut or rewrite these first.

## Empty framing

- `it's important to note`
- `in today's landscape`
- `let's dive into`
- `without further ado`
- `it's worth mentioning`

## Corporate fog

- `leverage`
- `robust`
- `seamless`
- `comprehensive`
- `innovative`
- `transformative`
- `holistic`

Replace with specific claims or simpler words.

## Artificial rhythm

Watch for:

- short dramatic fragments
- fake punchlines
- sentence pairs like `X. Not Y.`
- repetitive clause patterns

Rewrite into natural sentences with normal flow.

## False polish

- stacked adjectives
- empty intensifiers
- grand conclusions unsupported by the draft

Prefer one precise sentence over a decorated one.
